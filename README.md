# Copilot Studio Overview 101 - Both Harnesses

A 20-minute beginner-level presentation covering Microsoft Copilot Studio's Standard and GitHub Copilot harnesses, with industry-specific examples for provider, payer, and MedTech/pharma audiences.

## View the Deck

👉 **[Open Presentation](https://sunelt13.github.io/copilot-studio-overview101-both-harnesses/)**

## Audience Views

Use the selector in the top right to retarget the whole deck. Examples, presenter notes, the scenario grid, and the poll all change with it.

| View                   | Focus                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Provider** (default) | Health systems, hospitals, clinics: referrals, patient access, revenue cycle, discharge planning         |
| **Payer**              | Health plans: prior auth, claims, member services, appeals, care management                              |
| **MedTech & Pharma**   | Life sciences: complaint reportability, field service, medical information, MLR pre-check, trial support |

Link straight to a view with `?audience=provider`, `?audience=payer`, or `?audience=medtech`. Your last choice is remembered locally.

## Theme

Light and dark themes are both supported. The deck follows your OS setting by default; use the sun/moon button in the top right or press **T** to override. Your choice is remembered and reflected in the URL, so `?theme=light` forces the light deck - useful for a washed-out projector.

## Navigation

- **Scroll wheel** up/down to move between slides
- **Arrow keys** (← →, ↑ ↓) or **Space**, or click the left/right thirds of the slide
- **O** for the slide overview grid, **Esc** to close it
- **N** to toggle presenter notes
- **T** to toggle light/dark
- **1** / **2** / **3** to switch audience
- **Home** / **End** to jump to first/last slide
- Click anywhere on the progress bar to jump
- Swipe on mobile

## Content

- 17 slides covering LLMs, RAG, the three Copilot Studio harnesses, building blocks, orchestration, and industry use cases
- Audience-aware scenario grid with the recommended harness for each scenario
- Interactive poll slide
- Full presenter notes on every slide, rewritten per audience on the key slides

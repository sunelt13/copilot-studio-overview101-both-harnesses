What Are Agents?

An agent is a specialised AI assistant designed for specific tasks in your organisation.

📚

Knows Your Data

Clinical protocols, formularies, payer policies — grounded in your organisation's actual information, not generic web knowledge

⚡

Takes Action

Submits prior authorisations, routes referrals, updates patient records — real work, not just answers

🧠

Maintains Context

Remembers the conversation across turns — critical during complex patient intake or multi-step claims resolution

🔧

Low-Code

Drag-and-drop — clinical informaticists and operations teams can build and deploy without engineering

The AI Foundation: LLMs & RAG

Two concepts that power every agent.

Large Language Models (LLMs)

  • Prediction engine — Trained on massive text; predicts the next best token (word fragment)
  • Context window — How much the model can "hold in mind" at once
  • Instructions matter — Clear prompts steer better responses than clever phrasing

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

Ask
User query
"Formulary status of Drug X?"
→
Retrieve

Searches payer formulary DB
→
Augment
Add context
Appends retrieved data
→
Generate
Grounded answer
With citations to source
Key distinction: Knowledge informs answers (read-only). Tools take actions (write). Keep these separate — it saves grief later.

What Changed: Side by Side

Two architectures, different strengths.

Authored orchestration
📋

Standard Harness

You script the experience and keep tight control over the route.

  • You provide: Topics, rules, and conversation paths
  • At runtime: Follows maker-defined routing and paths
  • Primary control: Topics tab — trigger phrases, node graphs, branching
  • Orchestration: Classic (you route) or generative (AI selects topics)
  • Capabilities: Prompts, branches, structured Q&A
Goal-driven reasoning
🧠

GitHub Copilot Harness

You define the environment, and the agent reasons through the work.

  • You provide: Goals, instructions, knowledge, tools, and boundaries
  • At runtime: Plans how to pursue the goal, adjusts dynamically
  • Primary control: Instructions — natural language, no conversation trees
  • Orchestration: Goal-driven (plan → act → observe → replan)
  • Capabilities: Skills, memory, connected agents, file handling, MCP, Work IQ
Important: Agents cannot be transferred between harnesses — they use different architectures. Choose deliberately at the start.
